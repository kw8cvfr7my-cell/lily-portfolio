# Information Architecture

## Recruiter-first journey

```text
Landing / Explorer Hub
├── About Lily and AI Marketing Builder positioning
├── Education and core capabilities
├── Optional public CV/contact actions (only after Lily confirms assets/details)
└── Island exploration
    ├── Insight Forest — how Lily approaches user research, based on Small-U experience
    ├── AI Projects — three projects with clear stage labels
    └── Career Harbor — three selected roles and the capabilities each demonstrates
```

The four portfolio areas are **About Lily**, **Insight Forest**, **AI Projects**, and **Career Harbor**. About Lily is the home page/profile; the other three are island destinations available directly without requiring scene exploration. This is a user-requested structure, not the source Skill's default student categories.

## Module content

### About Lily

Introduce Lily Yang as an **AI Marketing Builder** moving from Marketing and Operations toward AI Product Building. Show her Zhejiang University of Water Resources and Electric Power undergraduate background, University of Leeds International Marketing MSc, and the agreed capability themes: User Research, Product Thinking, Growth Marketing, AI Product Operations, and Data Analysis. Use the line “Building AI-powered products through user insights and marketing strategy.”

### Insight Forest

Tell the Small-U story as a research and product-operations case: discover a problem, collect user needs, analyze feedback, identify patterns, and support system improvements. The current notes cite 50+ needs and 7 optimization directions. Tie claims to the user's de-identified evidence and distinguish Lily's contribution from team outcomes.

### AI Projects

Show only these three product stories:

1. **Small-U Feedback Assistant** — status provided by Lily: Prototype. Explain why it is being built, who it serves, how the prototype works, and its roadmap. Confirm the demonstrable scope before using screenshots or describing implemented functionality.
2. **GameLingo AI** — the current direction is a Player Insight Engine that analyzes Steam reviews and Reddit discussions to identify player pain points. Treat as a concept unless an implementation is confirmed. The supplied older localization-checking PRD does not match this new direction and must not be used as the current feature definition.
3. **Inspiration AI** — an AI Marketing Strategy Generator. Inputs: brand, audience, and goal. Intended outputs: campaign idea, growth strategy, and content plan. Label as a concept until its stage is confirmed.

### Career Harbor

Show only Joy Media (AI Product Operations / User Research), ZEEKR (SQL / Product Optimization / Growth Activities), and DiDi (User Operations / Product Operations). Present capability evidence, not a long responsibility list. Confirm metrics, dates, contributions, and any public-use limits before publishing details.

## Content panel pattern

Each item presents its status and context first, then Lily's role, actions, evidence, outcome, and reflection where confirmed. Team results are separated from individual contribution. Links and images appear only when supplied and approved. No raw internal feedback or private evidence link appears on the public site.

## Navigation behavior

The landing page plus three scene categories form the same four-area portfolio journey. A visitor can open any island destination without walking through the scene. The map, scene, menu, and explorable-area count use one shared data source.

## Bilingual structure

English is the default language for the UK recruiting audience. Chinese content is available through an explicit language switch. Names, metrics, dates, qualifications, and claim status retain the same meaning in both locales.

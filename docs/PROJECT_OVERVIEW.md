# Project Overview

## Purpose

AI Explorer Island is an interactive portfolio for recruiter and hiring-manager review. It presents Lily Yang's evidence and direction as an **AI Marketing Builder**, combining user insight, AI product thinking, growth marketing, and product operations.

## Audience and promise

Recruiters are the primary audience. Hiring managers and potential collaborators are secondary audiences. The first screen should quickly answer who Lily is, what she has done, what she is building, and where to find the strongest evidence.

## Experience principles

- Put a clear professional introduction, featured work, and CV access before optional exploration.
- Keep the portfolio useful without 3D, sound, or completing an exploration quest.
- Make each claim traceable to a supplied source or an explicitly confirmed statement.
- Distinguish completed work, work in progress, concepts, team outcomes, and personal contribution.
- Support English and Chinese throughout the main navigation and content.
- Use exploration as a light layer around professional evidence, not as a game-first objective.

## Portfolio modules

- **About Lily**: Lily Yang, AI Marketing Builder, education, capabilities, and concise introduction.
- **Insight Forest**: a user-research and product-operations story grounded in her Small-U experience.
- **AI Projects**: three stories only—Small-U Feedback Assistant, GameLingo AI, and Inspiration AI—with honest stage labels.
- **Career Harbor**: selected evidence from Joy Media, ZEEKR, and DiDi, organized around capabilities rather than a long resume.

The Small-U Feedback Assistant is currently labelled **Prototype** based on Lily's latest update; demonstrable scope and artifacts still need confirmation. GameLingo AI is currently framed as a player insight concept, which differs from the supplied localization-checking PRD. Inspiration AI is a concept unless its implementation status changes.

## Status

**MVP published on GitHub Pages.** The design, content structure, bilingual interface, static build, and public deployment are in place. Project stage and evidence remain clearly separated so concepts are not presented as finished products.

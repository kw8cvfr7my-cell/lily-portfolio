# Design System

## Design intent

Present a calm, credible AI product portfolio with a sense of exploration. Professional content leads; island scenery provides context and delight.

## Visual direction

- Preserve the source template's forest, sandbar, and shallow-sea themes.
- Use readable paper-like content panels and consistent spacing.
- Favor clear labels, evidence cards, and restrained motion over decorative effects.
- Keep contrast and text size readable across desktop and mobile.

## Components

- Persistent top navigation: identity, language switch, CV/contact actions.
- Featured project card: status, problem, role, evidence, next step.
- Four section cards with matching scene labels and map destinations.
- Content viewer with image zoom/close controls where images are supplied.
- Visible theme, sound, and global-view controls.

## Motion and accessibility

Respect reduced-motion settings, ensure keyboard access to all content, and keep audio off until requested. Provide a readable category interface that works without WebGL.

## Responsive behavior

On small screens, prioritize the introduction and category navigation; avoid horizontal overflow and make long content scroll within accessible, clearly bounded panels.
